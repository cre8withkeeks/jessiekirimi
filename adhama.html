Adhama Bridal Studio

From Announcement to Afterglow: Full-Cycle Event Marketing for Adhama Bridal Studio

The project

Adhama Bridal Studio’s event, Becoming the Bride, took place on 31 October 2026, for women preparing for their weddings. I took on the marketing and content from the first announcement on 24 September, through event-day coverage, into the post-event period: 38 days from announcement to event. All promotion was organic, with no ad spend.

My role

I handled creative direction, the content strategy and calendar, shooting and editing the Reels, scheduling and publishing, event-day coverage and the post-event storytelling plan. I planned the kind of carousels the campaign needed, then scheduled and posted them. Someone else designed them.

Pre-event24 September to 30 October. Most of the Reels went out here, following the content calendar.
Event day31 October. I shot the footage that documented the day.
Post-eventThree more Reels are planned to keep the story going. They are not published yet.

The content

14Reels across the campaign
5carousels I planned, scheduled and posted

Two Reel series: experiences previous brides have been through, and pro tips from wedding industry professionals. Also: who the event is for, a Dear Bride week Reel, the planning team preparing for event day, and an explainer on what a ticket includes.
