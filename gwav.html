Case study

GWAV Church

Beyond Content Creation: Planning, Producing and Coordinating Digital Stories

The project

GWAV, short for Growing With A Vision, is the youth church of PCEA Kahawa Sukari. I have been on its content team since 2024. The page builds an online community, makes people aware of what the church runs, and helps people grow in their spiritual disciplines.

My role

I lead the content team. I wrote the team brief and rota, assigned roles across creative direction, filming, editing and page management, and run regular review meetings. I also make content myself: I plan, direct and edit Reels, and I post them.

How the work runs

Planning happens at the start of each month. Most shooting happens on Sundays, guided by a shotlist for before, during and after service. A team of editors cuts the content, mainly short-form video, and then the social media managers post and reply to comments and messages.
