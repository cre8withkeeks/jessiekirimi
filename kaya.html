Case study

Kaya Collective

Building a Creative Community: The Kaya Collective Journey

The project

Kaya Collective is a band that tells stories about the Christian experience through story and song. Its events are musical storytelling evenings rooted in the word of God. The community is people walking a journey of faith together, plus music lovers who enjoy a well-made production.

The goal

The page exists to market the event. Instagram is the main place we do that, alongside individual WhatsApp statuses. I started it on 26 September 2024 and have run it since, working closely with Adriana Helga, the team’s leader and visionary. The work picks up as the annual event gets closer.

My role

I plan, shoot and edit the Reels, write captions, manage the page, and reply to comments and messages.

Genesis Reel: 7 follows, with 202 likes, 27 comments, 22 reposts, 19 shares and 4 saves. Meet the Sopranos (the first Kanaph in a Hat Reel): 185 likes, 5 comments, 13 reposts, 9 shares and 2 saves. The four-part series reached about 11,900 combined views (rounded). What is Kanaph?: 25 reposts.

Event attendance grew across three editions, with roughly 200 at the latest, the highest so far. That is the event’s growth, and this page does not claim it caused it.

Figures are from Instagram Insights and the profile page. The yearly range and the What is Kanaph? figure come from a 2 November 2026 capture. Views are not the same as reach.
