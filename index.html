About me

A good story gets people curious enough to show up.

I’m a self-taught social media marketer and event content creator with about two years of hands-on experience. I manage pages, plan content and make short-form video, mostly for events, communities and churches. I helped build Kaya Collective’s Instagram page from the start, led a content team at GWAV Church, and ran a 38-day campaign for an Adhama Bridal Studio event, from announcement to event day. I’m open to learning, growing and collaborating.
